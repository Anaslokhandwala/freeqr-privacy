Privacy Policy

Free QR Generator · Effective 1 October 2026

This policy explains how the Free QR Generator mobile app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The app lets you paste a link, turn it into a QR code, and save that QR code to your device. It is free and supported by ads.

Summary

  • You don’t need an account, and we don’t ask for your name, email, or any other personal details.
  • Links you enter and the QR codes you create stay on your device. We don’t send them to our servers, and we don’t have servers that collect them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information the app handles on your device

  • Links you enter. These are used only to draw the QR code on your screen. They are not stored after you close the app, and they are not sent anywhere.
  • QR code images. When you tap Download, the QR code is saved as an image to your device’s photo library or gallery. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

  • Photos / storage: to save the QR code image to your photo library or gallery when you tap Download. The app doesn’t read or upload your existing photos.
  • Internet: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (Android) and App Tracking Transparency (iOS): to let ads be personalised if you allow it. On iOS you are asked first, and you can say no.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show banner and full-screen ads. To show, measure, and protect ads from fraud, the AdMob software in the app may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device’s advertising ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • Information about your interactions with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash and performance data

This information is collected by Google under its own policies. We don’t receive personal information about you from Google. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the app, you are asked whether to allow personalised ads. Ads are only loaded after you make a choice.
  • iOS: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
  • If you decline personalisation, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Data retention and deletion

We don’t store any personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. QR code images you saved stay on your device until you delete them. For data collected by Google for advertising, see the Google links above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Communication between the app and Google’s ad services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
